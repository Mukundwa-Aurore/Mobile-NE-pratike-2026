Lexicon

Innovation

/ˌɪn.əˈveɪ.ʃən/

Noun

The action or process of innovating.

"The company's commitment to innovation has kept it at the forefront of the industry."

A new method, idea, product, etc.

"Technological innovations are changing the way we live and work."
Related Verb: Innovate

To make changes in something established, especially by introducing new methods, ideas, or products.

Synonyms

Revolution Modernization Creativity

Antonyms

Stagnation Custom Tradition

Etymology

Mid 16th century: from Latin innovatio(n-), from the verb innovare, from in- ‘into’ + novare ‘make new’ (from novus ‘new’).
